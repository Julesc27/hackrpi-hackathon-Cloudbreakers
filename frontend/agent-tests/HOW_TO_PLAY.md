# Cloudbreakers: How to Play

You are the captain of a small sky settlement. You will start from a saved town and receive a separate objective. Play the game in the browser to achieve it.

## Getting around

- The map shows your town and nearby islands. Select an island to see what it offers and which actions are available there.
- The left rail opens Build, the Captain's Log (quests), and Cloud's advice. The bottom navigation includes a map view. Close a panel or press Escape to return to the main map.
- The Captain's Log shows objectives, prerequisites, rewards, and progress. Its Continue Quest button helps you navigate but does not complete an action for you.
- Cloud offers suggestions. Check the game and the quest book to decide whether a suggestion helps your current objective.

## Taking actions

- Gather or collect resources on islands when those actions are available. New Haven has a limited local supply of wood and stone.
- Open Build to inspect buildings, upgrades, costs, and requirements. Some buildings need a free plot on a connected island; infrastructure upgrades may not need a plot. Follow the on-screen prompt after choosing an item.
- Explore islands and bridge routes on the map to expand the settlement. Bridge options show their costs and requirements before you build.
- Successful gameplay actions advance production and consumption and grant experience. Opening a panel or reading a quest does not advance the town. Quests can complete automatically when their conditions are met.
- If a control is disabled, check its cost, unlock requirement, and the resources shown in the game. If the tutorial overlay is visible, follow its prompt first.

Use the visible game controls to play. Do not edit the save, run code in the page, or call internal game functions. Check your progress in the UI before finishing.
